About Me

I am a Postdoctoral Research Associate in the Materials Theory Group at the University of Cambridge. Before joining Cambridge, I received my PhD from the Tsung-Dao Lee Institute at Shanghai Jiao Tong University.

My research focuses on strongly correlated quantum materials and their emergent broken-symmetry phases, with particular interests in unconventional superconductivity, charge and orbital order, transport properties, electron–lattice dynamics, and disorder effects in materials. She develops and applies theoretical and computational approaches, including density functional theory, dynamical mean-field theory, exact diagonalization, numerical canonical transformations, and model analysis, to understand quantum many-body phenomena in realistic materials.

Research Interests

  • Strongly correlated quantum materials and emergent broken-symmetry phases.
  • Unconventional superconductivity, charge order, orbital order, and intertwined phases.
  • Transport properties, electron–lattice dynamics, and disorder effects in materials.
  • Density functional theory, dynamical mean-field theory, exact diagonalization, numerical canonical transformations, and model analysis.
