About

Research notes, current projects, talks, and a focused literature workflow around quantum materials.

My research interests sit around strongly correlated quantum materials, especially systems where charge, spin, orbital, and lattice degrees of freedom remain deeply entangled.

I am particularly interested in kagome and layered materials, superconducting instabilities, charge order, magnetism, and the bridge between first-principles calculations and effective Hamiltonians.

This site collects my research directions and keeps a live arXiv reading page focused on cond-mat.str-el and cond-mat.supr-con.
